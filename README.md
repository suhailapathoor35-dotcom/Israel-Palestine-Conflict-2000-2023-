# Israel-Palestine Conflict (2000-2023)
## Indroduction
This report provides a data-driven analysis of the Israel-Palestine conflict between the years 2000 and 2023.
Approximately 11,000 fatalities have been recorded during this period. The average age of affected individuals is 26.70 years.
## Key Findings:

Primary Causes of Death: Gunfire is the most frequent cause of injury and death, accounting for approximately 91.15% of cases. Other significant causes include explosions, shelling, and stabbing.

Regional Impact: The Gaza Strip has been the most heavily affected region, followed by the West Bank and Israel.

Demographic Analysis: The majority of fatalities are Palestinian citizens, specifically adult males. By gender, 87.21% of victims are male, while 12.79% are female.

Responsible Parties: Data indicates that the majority of fatalities are attributed to actions by Israeli security forces.

Timeline of Hostilities: A significant spike in fatalities occurred in 2014. Detailed incident logs from that year show many casualties resulted from missile strikes and live ammunition during military engagements.
